## General assets directory

**structure**:


```none
|-- /css (software custom stylization)
|
|-- /tpl (HTML fragments)
|
|-- /js (software core script and custom function)
|     |-- /modules (ES6 modules)
|           |-- /core (application life cycle)
|           |-- /functions (general-purpose functions)
|           |-- /helpers (support utilities)
|           |-- /lib (project's own libraries)
|           |-- /models (posts, interface and control logic)
|           |-- /vendor (third-party libraries)
|
|-- /posts (Markdown files)
|     |-- /images (post images)
|
|-- /lang (software interface dictionaries)
|
|-- /media (audio, video and image assets)
|      |-- /audio
|      |-- /video
|      |-- /image (layout images)
|            |-- /icons (software or third-party icons)
|            |-- /logos (software or third-party logos)
|
|-- /vendor (used for development)
       |-- /... (multiple vendors)
```
